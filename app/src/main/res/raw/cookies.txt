COOKIES POLICY — RippleUp

Last updated: 20 July 2026

1. Cookies set by RippleUp
None. RippleUp is a native Android app, not a website. The concept of browser cookies does not apply. There is no cookie banner because the app simply does not use them.

2. Local data
The simulator stores points, badges, and streaks in a local on-device database (Room). This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Third-party SDKs
This app bundles Google ML Kit for on-device QR scanning. ML Kit runs entirely on-device and does not transmit scan data to any server.

4. Your controls
Because RippleUp itself does not collect data or set cookies, no consent choice is required. You control all data via your phone's standard app settings and uninstall.
